About Us

Welcome to Smash & Drop! We are a friendly, recreational badminton club open to players of all skill levels. Whether you haven't picked up a racket in years or you play every week, you'll find a welcoming community here. Our focus is on having fun, getting a good sweat in, and enjoying the game.

When & Where

We meet twice a week for open play and casual matches.

  • Tuesdays: 7:00 PM - 9:00 PM
  • Thursdays: 7:30 PM - 9:30 PM

Location: The Community Sports Hall, 123 Main Street, Townsville.

What to bring: Indoor non-marking sports shoes, comfortable clothes, and a water bottle. We provide plastic shuttlecocks, and we have a few spare rackets if you don't own one yet!

Membership & Pricing

We keep our costs low so everyone can play.

  • Drop-in session: $5 per night
  • Monthly membership: $30 per month
  • First session is FREE!
